Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how TechSutraLabs Ventures (OPC) Private Limited ("TechSutra Labs Ventures", "we", "us") handles personal data collected through techsutralabs.com.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send: usually your name, email address, organisation and message.

How we use it

We use it only to respond to your enquiry and, where you engage us, to provide our services. We do not sell or rent personal data.

We keep enquiry correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose it was shared for, or as required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
