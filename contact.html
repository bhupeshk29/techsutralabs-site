FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you build AI or generative AI solutions for our specific needs?

Yes. We start with your business problem and data, identify where AI adds real value, and then build anything from a quick proof of concept to a production system: assistants on your own documents, ML prediction models, document data extraction or AI agents that automate workflows.

Is our data safe when you use AI?

Yes. We design AI solutions with access controls, data minimisation and human review. For sensitive data we can use private or on-premises models so your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Which AI models do you work with?

We are model-agnostic. We work with leading commercial models such as OpenAI, Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini, as well as open-source models that can be hosted privately, and choose based on your accuracy, cost and privacy needs.

How do we start working together?

Send us a short description of your requirement. We set up a discovery call, understand your goals and constraints, and then share a proposal with scope, timeline and cost.

Can your products be customised for our organisation?

Yes. Our solutions are configurable, and we also build custom extensions and integrations with your existing systems.

Do you deploy in the cloud or on our own servers?

Either. Our software runs in the cloud, in a private cloud, or on-premises on your own infrastructure, depending on your security and compliance needs.

Do you support software you did not build?

Yes. We take over maintenance and support of existing applications after an initial assessment of the code and environment.

How do you protect our data?

Role-based access, encryption, audit trails and secure development practices are standard. We can sign NDAs and data-processing agreements, and we support the requirements of India's DPDP Act, 2023.
